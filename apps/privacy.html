Privacy policy

Last updated: 28 October 2026

This policy applies to the web apps at mingzillastudio.co.uk/apps and their subdomains, for example Soundsgood. Ming Huang runs these apps.

Data that we collect

  • Google sign-in: when you sign in with Google, we receive your email address and your name. We use the email address to check that you have an account in the app. We receive no other Google data, and we do not read your Google contacts, mail or files.
  • Your content: the notes, files and other material that you add to the app.
  • Logs: the server keeps a log of sign-ins and refusals. A log line has the time, the email address and the result.
  • Cookie: after you sign in, the app sets one session cookie. It keeps you signed in. We do not use cookies for advertising or tracking.

How we use the data

We use the data only to let you sign in and to run the app. We do not sell your data. We do not share it with other parties, and we do not use it for advertising.

Where the data is kept

The data is kept on a server that Ming Huang controls. We keep your data while your account is active. When an account is removed, the account data is removed.

Google

The use of information that we receive from Google follows the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.
